# Cooked Finance — active product brief

## Product promise

Cooked Finance explains the real cost of running a home. A visitor can turn an
official label or measured-use figure into an annual cost, compare repair and
replacement on the same timeline, and save the next maintenance date locally.

The site is U.S.-first because its initial evidence depends on FTC, DOE, EIA,
ENERGY STAR, EPA, CPSC, USDA, and U.S. utility units.

## Public search layer

The first system set is refrigerator, freezer, clothes washer, electric dryer,
dishwasher, electric water heater, room air conditioner, and dehumidifier.

Each indexable system page must include:

1. The exact preferred energy input and where to find it.
2. A warning that standardized label use is not actual household use.
3. A useful running-cost workflow.
4. Model-aware, source-supported maintenance tasks without invented cadences.
5. Explicit owner-safe and professional-only boundaries.
6. A source panel and substantive review date.
7. A path to My Home or the repair-versus-replace scenario.

Do not generate ZIP-by-appliance, model-number, symptom, or keyword-variation
pages unless each page contains materially distinct verified information.
Personal results remain `noindex`.

## Calculation contract

- Electricity: annual kWh × entered price per kWh.
- Natural gas: annual therms × entered price per therm.
- Monthly values are annual totals divided by 12, not bill forecasts.
- Repair and replacement are compared as up-front price plus annual operating
  cost over one entered horizon.
- Neither tool predicts failure, remaining life, repair quality, safety,
  comfort, financing, rebates, or future repairs.
- Washer and dishwasher standardized kWh must disclose modeled hot-water energy
  to prevent double counting with a complete water-heater estimate.

Material engine changes require regression cases and a public methodology
version entry.

## Safety boundary

Cost never overrides a recall or hazard. Do not publish DIY instructions for
gas, refrigerant, sealed systems, internal 120/240-volt work, compressors,
active leaks near electricity, or pressurized relief devices. Smoke, burning
odor, shock, sparking, damaged cords/outlets, and recurring breaker trips route
to stopping use and qualified help.

## Privacy contract

Calculators run locally. Home records and legacy financial values must not be
placed in URLs or analytics events. My Home saves only after an explicit user
action and provides delete and export controls. Analytics remains consent-based.

## Monetization boundary

There are no ads today. Any later AdSense implementation must exclude forms,
personalized results, My Home, safety notices, source panels, methodology,
privacy, legal, corrections, and unfinished pages. Home-plan and calculator
values must never become advertising parameters or audiences.

## Legacy finance area

The original financial check, score methodology, guides, benchmark, and four
calculators remain educational archive tools. They keep their own formulas,
tests, privacy guarantees, and methodology and do not drive the new homepage.
